Activity 01

Pause Challenge

Homework → Pause → One Breath → Begin.

How it works

1

Before homework

Use the normal moment when your child is about to begin homework.

2

Pause

Take a brief pause before beginning.

3

One breath

Take one deep breath.

4

Begin

Start homework as usual.

What are we learning?

The activity is intended to help us observe whether a child can apply a simple pause-and-breathe practice at a naturally occurring moment, and what parents notice during the experience.

What to notice

  • Was your child able to try it?
  • How much prompting was needed?
  • What did you notice about your child's response?
  • What did you notice about your own response?
  • What felt easy, difficult, unusual, or unexpected?
