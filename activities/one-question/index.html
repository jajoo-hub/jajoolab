Activity 02

One Question

One small question. One honest conversation.

The activity

Invite your child to ask you one non-academic question each day, whenever it naturally fits into the day.

Answer honestly and openly. There is no need to force the conversation.

Some ideas

“What were you like as a child?”

“What sport did you like?”

“What was something funny that happened to you?”

“What was your favourite activity?”

There is no required outcome.

The purpose is to observe what actually happens: the questions children ask, how the interaction feels, what parents notice, and what unexpected experiences or friction appear.

What to notice

  • Did your child ask without much prompting?
  • Were there follow-up questions?
  • Did the conversation continue naturally?
  • Did your child share something?
  • Did you notice anything new?
  • How natural or difficult did it feel?
