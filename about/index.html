About

Exploring the gap between knowing and doing.

JajooLab is an initiative focused on practical ways children can apply healthy life skills when they are needed.

The problem we are interested in

Children are taught many useful life skills — such as deep breathing, listening, patience, self-control, and not giving up. They may also learn why those skills are useful.

The question here is different: what happens when the skill is actually needed, especially during a difficult or mildly emotional moment?

A practical approach

We create small activities, try them with families, and pay attention to what actually happens. Parent experience matters alongside what is observed about the child.

What this initiative is not

It is not a replacement for parents, schools, Yoga, or existing education. It is also not limited to breathing, and it does not promise psychological, academic, behavioural, or relationship benefits before they are demonstrated.

How we learn

Observe before concluding

Concrete experiences are more useful than assumptions about what should happen.

Keep it practical

Activities should be lightweight and compatible with real family life.

Unexpected results matter

Neutral, negative, incomplete, and unexpected experiences are useful learning.

Learn together

Parents are participants in the learning process, not just recipients of instructions.
